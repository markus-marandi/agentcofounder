My nephews are always borrowing comics off my shelf and I lose track of
who has what. I want to log each issue, its title, who the artist is, and
roughly what kind of comic it is, like a superhero one or something more
indie or a manga. When one of them borrows an issue I want to note down
their name, and when it comes back I want to clear that off. Mostly I
want to see my whole collection in one list, and be able to pick out just
the ones that are currently out with someone. It's just me keeping track,
from my own computer.
