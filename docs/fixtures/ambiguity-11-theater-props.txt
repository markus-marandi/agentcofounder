People in our community theater group keep borrowing props from my storage
room and I never remember who has what. I want to log each prop, its
name, which show it's from, and roughly what kind of prop it is, like a
costume piece or a set piece or something more of a hand prop. When
someone borrows one I want to note down their name, and when it comes
back I want to clear that off. Mostly I want to see everything I have in
one list, and be able to pick out just the ones that are currently out
with someone. It's just me keeping track, from my own computer.
